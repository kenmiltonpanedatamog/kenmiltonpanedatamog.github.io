"Is it better to speak, or to die?"

I ask myself that question more often than not. Lately, that phrase has become more relevant, as I currently battle unsaid thoughts and feelings for that matter.

Lately, I've been wanting to be with my only close friends, for whom I can tell about everything. Last Friday, that longing was so intense, that I thought about shifting a course, just to be with one of them. I never really had many friends for whom I can rant my problems to, and it makes me sad because a good friend is all I need to speak to.

I was feeling a mixture of feelings as of late. I feel like I'm lost, I feel like I've been disappointing myself, I feel like I want a new start, I feel like I want to just speak the unspeakable. All of these feelings never reached the ear or the eye of a person. Only my journal writings saw the struggle to even describe those feelings. Honestly, I just want to be loved, not by my family or friends, but by someone. That's actually one of the feelings I've felt as of late.

I thought, I would experience the start of something, when I somehow met (online) someone in a forum. We exchanged Instagrams and talked for several days, but it ended when that person stopped responding. We were supposed to meet so I can tour that person in a place. But that person just disappeared. Then, I grew a feeling, an early-stage feeling for someone in my school. I don't even know why because it felt so random. I never saw that person's face for more than 5 minutes, but the glimpse was enough to attract me. It's so weird and shallow to be honest, but who am I to say that what I'm feeling is not true?

Is it just a coping mechanism or reaction because of the stress and uncertainty I've been feeling since the school started? I don't know. I want to know.

So answer the question. Is it better to speak, or to die?

My answer would be to die, but I want, so badly to speak. But I'm so afraid. I want to speak to someone who could convince me that dying is not the option. I want a person, or a set of people, to listen when I do have the courage to speak. I want all of these but I don't have them right now.

(Disclaimer: The word 'dying' is used metaphorically here, as the original phrase meant.)
