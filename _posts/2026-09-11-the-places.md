I woke up one day after taking my afternoon nap. My lola told me to sleep in the afternoon for me to grow my height. Most of the time, I would just pretend to be sleeping but end up waking up a few hours later feeling like I was reborn. Most times, I slept in my lola and lolo’s house but there are times when they would visit their riverside cabin made of bamboos. I took a swim on the river most days, I created sand balls and cured them under the sun, and I helped pick fish that my lolo caught using a net. There is a steep road that led to the riverside and I would ride my toy car, sending me speeding downhill. 

The river had a wide flat of sand, rocks, and an assortment of grasses. It was a river that traversed between the mountains and stretched for what felt like infinity. I only went to part of the river near my grandparent's cabin. When I was six or seven, they built a small dam and an irrigation system which became another place for me to swim. On the dam, there is a 5 meter tower, which on top has the manual gears to control the dam's flow. The dam had a wide slide that made the water flow through it with a speed that creates momentum when you slide on it and also creates a higher pressure stream of water which sends them rushing though a flat cemented region before falling on the untouched river. The dam attracted kids. There is a place for the normal kids where they can jump on a small elevated area and swim back and forth on the dam. But there is also a place for those kids who already had the nerves to take risks. There are deep parts of the dam which is too deep for even adults, but the most dangerous part of it was the shallow opening for the irrigation system. You can enter this opening, which is so dark and narrow from the outside. I never had the courage to even enter that opening, some kids did it as I fear if they would ever come back, and to make matters worse, that opening will never allow a person to get back from the other side. I'd like to imagine it as a death opening, wherein a sudden rise of the river or a thing entering it which blocks the way back will deliver someone inside to an untimely death. I never heard of someone getting killed by that opening, but who knows what sort of things already happened inside it. The opening allowed the water to flow from the dam to the narrow irrigation system which attracted mothers and kids. The girls would do their laundry there while the kids play along this narrow opening with a constant flow of water passing by.  

My grandparents also had a mountain cabin, which is just a small resting place while my lolo cuts bamboos and my lola plants or harvests some edible plants. They sometimes take me hiking on the mountain. There is a cemented path where we walked, but it was mostly leaves and a lot of tree debris. There was nothing much to do on the mountains besides waiting for my grandparents to finish what they are doing, I would just eat a cracker and a soft drink while I wait for our eventual descent. Everyday was a fun day to go out and swim or hike, take a nap, and play the whole rest of the afternoon.

We live in my grandparents’ house. It is a two-story half cement, and half wooden house. Our room is in the ground floor, with my grandparents on the other room nearby. On the second floor are my titas. There is a wide space on the second floor where I would spend most of my time inside the house. There is a wooden staircase that led to a big door similar to the big church doors. It led to the front of the house and a view to the neighborhood. The walls is covered by picture frames and an altar lay at the center. I like to spend my time here reading or looking at pictures in my books. I have a set of encyclopedias of different topics and random picture books. I would fall asleep here, and would wake up from either the call for lunch or the arrival of my mother. My mother worked in the outskirts of our town as a public school teacher. My father meanwhile worked somewhere far away.

On Sundays, we went to the town proper, which we call locally as “ili”, to go to the church and go to the public market (“palengke”) afterwards. This Sunday ritual made me love to go out in this little town because we always had burgers before going home. On special occasions, we went to Agoo, a nearby town more developed than ours. There is a Jollibee, malls, and a lot of toys. On rare occasions, we went to Baguio, the nearest city from our home. We would ride my lolo’s tricycle to go to the town proper, we would ride a jeep to go to Agoo, and we would ride a bus to go to Baguio City. My titas took me to some other places as well, they would bring me to their school and have me sleep on their apartments.

My senses travel in these few places near home. But my experiences revolve around the few frequent places I spent my days. My grandparents' house, our neighborhood roads, and the river— these are my favorite places.
