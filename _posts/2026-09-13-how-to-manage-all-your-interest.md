## Make a Graveyard
- A list of thoughts that comes into mind, it could be a cool project, a want to buy, or anything that you want to do, but can't at the moment.

## Look for Life Multipliers
- Skills or activities that have an implication in other areas of life that will give you the most benefit.

## Make Your "Must-Do" List
- Similar to the idea of "Bucket List" where you write anything that you want to achieve or do in your lifetime. The items in the list may not last long, because interests come and go, but having a list gives you a road map or an overview on what your life goals are.

## Do a Time Audit
- Auditing how much we spend on something can benefit us know how much time we need to do something, and it also help us realize when we are wasting our time. For instance, knowing that we are spending hours watching TikTok can be a wake up call to us. Just like in finances, a time well audited can present a lot of opportunities for optimization and avoiding wasted resources, that is our time.

## Change Your S.U.P. (smallest unit of productive time)
- The SUP, or the smallest unit of productive time is a realistic time frame where you can have progress in a certain area of life or interest. For instance, an SUP for being mindful through meditation could be a 10 minute uninterrupted time where you really get to focus without any distractions. The SUP should be realistic and flexible because the days are not equal in a sense that one day could be a super productive day because you had a good sleep, while there could be lazy days where that SUP is not enough to even get focused.

## Avoid Zero Days
- Consistency is key. Do a little bit everyday because it builds a habit, even if the progress is small and marginal. Having zero days means you show up no matter what.
