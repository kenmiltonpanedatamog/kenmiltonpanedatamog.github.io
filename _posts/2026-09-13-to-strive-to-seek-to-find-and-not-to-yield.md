# How this quote captures my ultimate goals in life

To strive,
to seek,
to find,
and not to yield.
—"Ulysses" by Alfred Lord Tennyson

This line, from a poem "Ulysses" by Alfred Lord Tennyson, is one of my favorite quotations that lives rent free in my mind. It is a quote so powerful, that I consider it my life motto, and a quote I would likely want to engrave on my tombstone. It is not even a complete sentence, yet it captures the very idea of what a whole life should be.

We like a quote for many reasons; it sounds cool, it reflects an idea we like or our own personality, or it makes us better persons. The latter is the reason for me liking this quote.

When I think about this quote, I think of my ultimate goals in life:
- I want to improve myself through constant learning. I don't want to die knowing that I settled. I don't want myself to be that person who stops growing after college.
- I want to experience a lot of things. I don't want to live my life doing what is the default (study>work>have a family>work>retire). I don't want to die a slave, not having experienced the wonders of the world and my dreams.
- I want to find out, fail, and succeed. I want to explore what I want to do in life, I want to experience failure which will help me grow, and I want to succeed because I persevered.
- I want to be me. I want to live my life how I want it, to not let other people dictate my life's direction. I want to be proud of myself because it was me who dreamed, worked, and succeeded in my goals.

This quote perfectly advocates for a life of constant improvement and exploration. It advocates for finding our inner sense of identity and purpose. And it inspires us not to give up.
